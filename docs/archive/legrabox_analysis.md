## 10. SPECIAL APPLICATIONS

### 10.1 SPACE TOWER (Larder Unit)

- Cabinet width KB: 275–1200 mm
- Uses inner drawers (M) and inner pull-outs (C)
- Recommended: CLIP top BLUMOTION 155° hinge
- Flexible positioning of pull-outs at different heights

### 10.2 SPACE TWIN (Narrow Cabinet)

- Cabinet width KB: 185–250 mm
- Dynamic carrying capacity: 20 kg per base cabinet
- Uses diagonally offset cabinet profiles

### 10.3 Sink Cabinet

- Special cutouts for plumbing
- Available in M (drawer) and C (high fronted pull-out)

## 15. CRITICAL LIMITATIONS SUMMARY

1. **N height limited to NL 400–550** — cannot use for very short or very long drawers
2. **K height limited to NL 300–600** — not available for 270 or 650
3. **70 kg capacity only from NL 450+** — short runners are 40 kg only
4. **650 NL only 70 kg** — no 40 kg option for longest runners
5. **Design options exclude height N** — must use standard drawer side for N
6. **SPACE TWIN max 20 kg** — not 40 kg
7. **EXPANDO T requires min 6 mm front** — standard requires 12 mm
8. **Tolerance ±1.5 mm built-in** — account for this in cabinet construction
9. **Inner drawer requires latch set** — only from height M (71 mm) upwards
10. **Steel back not compatible with SERVO-DRIVE** — use chipboard back instead
