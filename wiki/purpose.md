# Wiki Purpose

This wiki covers: Projektowanie, sprzedaż i montaż mebli kuchennych. Standardy, materiały, ergonomia i instrukcje montażu..

Include: This wiki covers all aspects of designing, selling, and installing kitchen furniture, including materials, ergonomics, standards, and assembly instructions.
Exclude: This wiki does not cover general home renovation, appliance repair, or unrelated furniture types.
