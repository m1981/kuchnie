# CNC-Driven Furniture Production

This page details the principles and practices of furniture production leveraging Corpus LTR software and collaboration with CNC (Computer Numerical Control) services. It emphasizes a 'Shift-Left' strategy, prioritizing machine-based processing for efficiency and precision in furniture manufacturing.

## Best Practices for CNC Orders

When engaging with CNC services for furniture production, adherence to specific best practices is crucial for optimal results. These include:

- **Mandatory Drilling:** Ensuring all necessary drilling operations are clearly specified and executed by the CNC service. ^[Zasady_Projektowania_Corpus.md:13]
- **Use of PUR Glue:** Utilizing Polyurethane (PUR) glue for enhanced durability and bonding strength in furniture assembly. ^[Zasady_Projektowania_Corpus.md:14]

## Production Workflow

The workflow from design to client installation is streamlined through the integration of CNC technology. This process typically involves:

1.  **Design Phase:** Utilizing software like Corpus LTR to create detailed furniture designs.
2.  **Data Preparation:** Generating precise manufacturing data, potentially including formats like [[dxf-files]] for CNC machines. ^[Zasady_Projektowania_Corpus.md:16]
3.  **CNC Machining:** Executing cutting, drilling, and shaping operations using CNC equipment. ^[Zasady_Projektowania_Corpus.md:35]
4.  **Assembly:** Assembling the manufactured components, often incorporating [[akcesoria-do-mebli-kuchennych]].
5.  **Client Installation:** Final installation of the furniture at the client's location. ^[Zasady_Projektowania_Corpus.md:41]

## Materials and Software

## Business Model for Custom Kitchens with CNC Outsourcing

This document outlines a business model for a sole proprietor (JDG) specializing in custom kitchens. The model leverages CNC outsourcing for manufacturing and emphasizes a 'Shift-Left' design principle, prioritizing machine-based processing for efficiency and precision. It details a series of playbooks covering client qualification, measurement, sales, design, ordering, and installation. ^[Playbook_System.md:1,2,3]

### Key Playbooks and Processes:

- **Client Qualification:** Initial screening to ensure project suitability.
- **Measurement:** Accurate on-site measurements for custom designs.
- **Sales:** Strategies for presenting proposals and closing deals.
- **Design:** Utilizing tools like [[corpus-ltr]] for efficient design and preparation for CNC.
- **Ordering:** Streamlining the process of sending specifications to CNC services.
- **Installation:** Efficient and precise assembly of kitchen furniture.

This approach aims to maximize efficiency and precision by integrating technology and a structured workflow throughout the kitchen furniture lifecycle.
