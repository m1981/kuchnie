# 5K Framework for Kitchen Design

The "5K" framework (Klatka, Kotwice, Klocki, Korekty, Kontrola) offers a structured methodology for designing kitchen furniture. This approach breaks down the design process into distinct, manageable stages to ensure a functional, safe, and efficient kitchen layout. ^[00-system-5k.md:71]

## The 5K Stages:

1.  **Klatka (Cage/Zone):** This initial stage focuses on defining the primary working zones within the kitchen. It involves identifying and demarcating areas for specific activities, ensuring a safe and logical flow of movement. This is akin to establishing the fundamental layout and boundaries for the kitchen's functional spaces. ^[00-system-5k.md:73]
2.  **Kotwice (Anchors):** In this phase, critical elements of the kitchen are strategically placed. These "anchors" typically include the [[zlew]] (sink), [[plyta]] (hob/stove), and potentially the [[lodowka]] (refrigerator) and [[okapy]] (hoods). Their placement is crucial for establishing the core workflow and ergonomics of the kitchen, often building upon the zones defined in the "Klatka" stage. ^[00-system-5k.md:64]
3.  **Klocki (Blocks/Cabinets):** Once the primary zones and anchor points are established, this stage involves filling the remaining space with cabinets and other furniture elements. This is where the practical aspects of storage and cabinetry are integrated into the design, ensuring that all available space is utilized effectively and aesthetically. ^[00-system-5k.md:25]
4.  **Korekty (Corrections/Adjustments):** This is a critical review and refinement stage. It involves conducting "collision tests" and other checks to identify and resolve any potential issues with the design. This might include ensuring sufficient clearance between elements, checking for ergonomic conflicts, and verifying that all components fit together as intended before proceeding to manufacturing. ^[00-system-5k.md:31]
5.  **Kontrola (Control/Preparation for Manufacturing):** The final stage focuses on preparing the design for production, particularly for [[cnc-driven-furniture-production]]. This involves ensuring all specifications, measurements, and drilling instructions are precise and ready for machine processing. It's the final quality check and handover to the manufacturing phase. ^[00-system-5k.md:40]
